Demo mode replays week {{ active }} as a mid-Sunday afternoon: the early games final, the afternoon slate in the fourth quarter, the late slate and the night games still to come. Real players, real lineups, real projections — only the clock and the points are invented.

It works by replacing what Sleeper says, not by writing invented history. The one exception is a handful of curses, tokens, blessings and marks, which have to be real rows to show up at all; every one is flagged and deleted when you switch demo off.

Goosifer’s Wrath is staged in all three states so you can confirm it by looking: one owner marked and cursed right now (top of the Curse board, flame edge, ribbon), one who already paid — a spent mark and the doubled chugs it produced, sitting in Chugs above, where confirming either one mints nothing — and one carrying a token earned by surviving a curse.

Auto-lock and auto-settle stop while demo mode is on. Four of those games say FINAL, and settling against them would raise real chugs off invented scores.
